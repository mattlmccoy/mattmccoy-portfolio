Running Programs Without Structure in Ambiguous Settings

The job description specifically calls out experience in "ambiguous or high-stakes settings" and draws a parallel to management consulting backgrounds. My doctoral research at Georgia Tech is the clearest example: I pitched the concept for Radio Frequency Additive Manufacturing, secured approximately $150,000 in internal research funding plus approximately $170,000 from the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship, and built the first RFAM platform from the ground up within a year.
